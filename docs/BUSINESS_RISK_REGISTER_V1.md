# Property OS — Business Risk Register v1

Scale:
Likelihood: Low / Medium / High
Impact: Low / Medium / High / Critical

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Early signal | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Core retention fails | Medium | Critical | properties created but no return usage | narrow JTBD, building/lease events, kill features |
| Scope too broad | High | High | many half-built modules | release gates; no R3/R4 before R1 evidence |
| Incumbent copies features | Medium | Medium | similar property-management additions | history/trust/collaboration depth |
| Paid CAC too high | Medium | High | paid users don't retain | referral/building/content first |
| Inflation destroys pricing | High | High | support/vendor cost reprices faster than ARPA | frequent pricing review, short commitments |
| Security/privacy incident | Medium | Critical | anomalous access / leaked PII | least privilege, audit, encryption, incident plan |
| Official integration unavailable | High | High | partner/API delays | remain valuable without official rail; adapters |
| Legal mis-positioning | Medium | Critical | user assumes internal state is official | labels, capability gates, legal review |
| Inspection quality variance | Medium | High | rework/disputes | templates, QA sampling, evidence, training |
| Founder bandwidth | High | High | support + coding + sales context switching | explicit WIP limits, trigger-based hiring |
| Key-person dependency | High | High | secrets/process known by one founder | shared admin, runbooks, 2FA, backups |
| Weak monetization | Medium | High | high activity but low conversion | pricing interviews, package by value |
| Building manager resistance | Medium | Medium | onboarding stalls | import/simple first charge cycle |
| User data quality low | High | Medium | inconsistent records | progressive completeness + evidence model |
| Payment/tax admin delay | Medium | Medium | monetization launch blocked | setup ahead of paid launch |
| Talent loss / hiring difficulty | Medium | High | long vacancies | remote/flexible, docs, contractor bench |
