# Property OS — Capital Allocation & Funding Strategy v1

## Principle

Do not raise or spend against the final vision before proving the recurring wedge.

## Bootstrapped / pre-seed priorities

Spend first on:
1. secure production foundation
2. user research
3. product implementation
4. minimal reliable infrastructure
5. SMS/support essentials
6. specialist legal/security advice at decision points

Delay:
- broad paid marketing
- large office
- full-time sales team
- large field workforce
- expensive data/AI infrastructure without product use
- non-core enterprise tooling

## Fundraising readiness

Investor evidence pack should include:
- founder agreement / IP
- product demo
- retained cohorts
- MAMP growth
- collaboration rate
- paid tests
- CAC experiments
- unit economics assumptions
- market research methodology
- regulatory path
- security architecture
- 18-month use of funds

## Funding decision gate

Raise external capital when capital can accelerate a repeatable engine:
- proven retention + distribution
or
- regulated/field network needs justified by validated demand

Do not raise solely to finance unresolved PMF.

## Scenario budget

Maintain:
- Base plan
- Inflation stress plan
- Regulatory delay plan
- Growth acceleration plan

Review monthly while macro volatility remains high.
