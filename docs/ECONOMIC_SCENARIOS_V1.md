# Property OS — Economic & Market Scenarios v1

Research snapshot: 2026-09-23

## Current evidence

### Digital reach
Digital 2026 reports:
- 73.8M internet users in Iran at end-2025
- 79.6% internet penetration
- 151M cellular connections
- 48M social-media user identities

Implication:
Mobile-first/PWA/Android distribution is reasonable; SMS remains strategically useful for identity and critical operational communication.

### Population
World Bank:
- Iran 2025 population ~92.4M

### Macro pressure
World Bank 2025:
- GDP growth negative
- annual consumer inflation above 40%

2026 CPI releases indicate materially higher inflation during the year.

Implication:
Never build financial plans assuming stable nominal costs or stable consumer subscription willingness.

## Business scenarios

### S1 Stabilization
Assumptions:
- inflation moderates
- household planning improves
- payment and business operations stabilize

Strategy:
- annual plans
- paid owner SaaS
- marketplace experiments

### S2 Persistent inflation
Assumptions:
- high inflation
- cost repricing
- consumer pressure

Strategy:
- low burn
- frequent price reviews
- emphasize financial loss prevention
- building/group pricing
- avoid fixed multi-year contracts

### S3 Regulatory opening
Assumptions:
- private platform access to official real-estate transaction services becomes commercially accessible

Strategy:
- integration partner
- compliance/security
- gated official workflows
- no unsupported claims of authority

### S4 Regulatory friction
Assumptions:
- private integration slow or restricted

Strategy:
- operational OS remains standalone
- evidence packs
- guided handoff to official channels
- no dependency in R1/R2 economics

## Financial planning rules

- Maintain runway in months, not only nominal IRR budget.
- Reforecast monthly in high volatility.
- Separate:
  - fixed burn
  - inflation-sensitive local costs
  - usage-based infra
  - acquisition spend
  - field ops
- Price experiments should measure willingness to pay in current purchasing-power conditions.
