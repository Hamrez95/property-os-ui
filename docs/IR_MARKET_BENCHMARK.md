# Iran Property / Short‑Stay Benchmark v1

Research snapshot: 2026-09-22

## Purpose
This benchmark is **coverage research**, not a requirement to copy competitor IA or visual design.

Property OS keeps these layers separate:
1. Property Record — relatively stable physical/legal facts
2. Relationship — Party + Role + Scope + Time + Ownership Share
3. Listing Projection — sale/rent presentation and market terms
4. Short-Stay Offering — nightly operation, calendar, capacity and rules
5. Claim + Evidence — facts requiring provenance/verification

## Key market coverage
### Apartment
Area; rooms; floor/total floors; age/year; parking; storage; elevator; balcony; heating/cooling; unit count; occupancy; transaction terms.

### Villa / House
Land + built area; floors; master rooms; bathrooms; yard/garden; parking capacity; utilities; pool/jacuzzi; terrace/roof; privacy; access; permit/end-of-work.

Short-stay belongs in a separate offering profile:
capacity, maximum guests, beds/mattresses, check-in/out, minimum stay, daily pricing calendar, instant booking, house rules, pet/event/smoking policies, reviews and host operations.

### Commercial
Area; frontage/shopfront; door/front count; ceiling height; warehouse; restroom; 3-phase electricity; loading access; ownership vs sarqofli/right.

### Office
Area; rooms; floor; official/actual use; elevators; parking; storage; kitchenette; meeting room; HVAC; network/fiber; access hours; security.

### Land
Area; dimensions; frontage; road width; number of fronts/corners; orientation; slope/shape; registered use; utilities; access; deed/share.
Density, setback and buildability are Claims until sourced.

### Old / Teardown
Land area; current built area; building age/condition; current floors/units; occupancy; frontage; roads; utilities; old permits/documents; demolition access.
Participation-in-construction is an Offering, not a Property fact.

## Product ideas worth preserving
- Kilid: map draw, market heatmap, valuation trend as later analytics/marketplace features.
- Otaghak/Shab: host calendar, instant booking, transaction history, request management.
- Jabama: amenity-rich filtering, accessibility/pet/event filters, dynamic pricing.
- Jajiga: nearby/map search and capacity/type/rate/climate filtering.

## Guardrail
Do not turn the canonical Property table into a union of every listing and booking field. Model projections/operations separately.
