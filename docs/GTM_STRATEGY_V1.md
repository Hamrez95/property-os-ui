# Property OS — Go-to-Market & Marketing Strategy v1

## 1. GTM thesis

Do not launch nationally as a marketplace.

Start with a narrow, observable workflow where a small team can personally understand failure:
**owners + residents + building managers in Tehran.**

## 2. ICP priority

### ICP 1 — Small urban owner
- 1–3 properties
- currently uses chat/paper/notes
- has tenant/lease/documents
- feels renewal/maintenance/admin pain

### ICP 2 — Building manager
- 8–40 units
- charge/expense/announcement pain
- resident communication fragmented
- not an enterprise property manager

### ICP 3 — Multi-property owner
- 4–20 properties
- spreadsheets / assistants
- wants expiries, occupancy and finance overview

## 3. Founder-led acquisition

First 100 activated properties:
- founders personally onboard
- watch every failure
- no broad ad campaign
- capture source/channel
- 20–30 minute onboarding max target

## 4. Activation

A signup is not activation.

Activated property:
- created/imported
AND at least one:
- lease added
- document added
- role invitation accepted
- charge cycle created
- maintenance item created

## 5. Content engine

### Search/SEO
High-intent topics:
- تمدید قرارداد اجاره
- مدارک ملک
- شارژ ساختمان
- وظایف مدیر ساختمان
- پارکینگ و انباری
- تحویل ملک
- تعمیرات دوره‌ای
- چک‌لیست مالک
- چک‌لیست مستأجر
- سند و سهم دانگ

Each article should have a product action:
"این مورد را برای ملک خود ثبت/یادآوری کن."

### Social
Short educational stories:
problem → risk → simple framework → Property OS workflow.

### Calculators/tools
- rent/deposit organizer
- building charge calculator
- lease countdown
- maintenance checklist
- property document checklist

These can generate organic leads.

## 6. Referral loops

### Relationship invite
Owner → tenant
Tenant → owner/manager
Manager → whole building
Owner → representative/family

### Referral incentive
Avoid cash-first incentives initially.
Use product value:
- extra document storage
- verification discount
- additional property slot
- export/report

## 7. Partnership channels

Priority:
1. independent building managers
2. maintenance/service vendors
3. inspectors
4. legal/notary advisors for credibility/content
5. property professionals once owner records exist
6. insurers later

## 8. Paid marketing gate

Do not scale paid spend until:
- activation is stable
- 30/90 day retention understood
- referral baseline measured
- first paid conversion proven

Then test:
- search intent
- retargeting
- narrowly segmented social campaigns

## 9. Messaging by segment

Tenant:
"قرارداد، شارژ، تعمیرات و پیام‌های ساختمانت یکجا."

Owner:
"ملکت را فراموش نکن؛ قرارداد، مدارک، تعمیرات و مستأجر را مدیریت کن."

Manager:
"شارژ، بدهی، هزینه، اعلان و تعمیرات ساختمان شفاف و قابل پیگیری."

Multi-owner:
"تمام املاکت در یک پنل؛ اشغال، قرارداد، مالی و ریسک."

Trust:
"هر ادعا یک منبع؛ هر تأیید قابل توضیح."

## 10. CRM/lifecycle

Key lifecycle events:
- onboarding incomplete
- property created
- invite pending
- lease expiry 90/60/30/7 days
- charge due
- unpaid charge
- maintenance due
- verification expiring
- listing stale

Channels:
- in-app
- push
- SMS for critical/OTP/service communication
- email for reports/export where useful

Marketing consent separate from operational messaging.

## 11. Experiments backlog

- manager-led building onboarding vs owner-led
- free one-property limit vs free one-role scope
- annual vs monthly pricing
- family/representative invite
- PDF property report as viral artifact
- verified passport as seller acquisition
- property checklist SEO
