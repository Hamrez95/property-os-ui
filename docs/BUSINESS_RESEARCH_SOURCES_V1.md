## Strategic / ecosystem research

### 2025 Strategic Change paper on Iranian entrepreneurial ecosystem
Signal:
network position, accelerators/incubators and collaboration matter; later-stage capital network appears weaker.

### 2025 Journal of Innovation & Knowledge study
Signal:
Iran startup commercialization is constrained by follow-on funding, talent depth, ecosystem maturity and international restrictions.

Use:
supports lean burn, investment-readiness discipline and trigger-based hiring—not a claim that all startups face identical outcomes.

## Research policy

A source supports **context**, not automatic strategy.

Every strategic claim in fundraising/board material should distinguish:
- sourced fact
- calculation
- interpretation
- founder hypothesis
- approved decision

Do not cite a market-size number unless its unit, date, population and formula are reproducible.
