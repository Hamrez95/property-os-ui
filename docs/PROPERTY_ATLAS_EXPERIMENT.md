# Property Atlas — visual home experiment

Status: Prototype / founder decision surface

## Purpose

An optional visual layer over Property OS: it makes a user's related Properties, Spaces and Building mandate easier to remember without replacing operational Home, search, lists or alerts.

## Guardrails

- It is **not** a city-builder or game loop.
- It is derived from Property Record + RoleAssignment + Scope; a listing never becomes the canonical visual object.
- The active Role / Scope controls which markers are emphasised in a production implementation.
- Urgent operations remain visible as ordinary cards and notifications outside the scene.
- Motion is ambient only and disabled by `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Prototype routes

- `#/app/atlas-welcome` — splash / first-impression direction
- `#/app/atlas` — interactive asset-neighbourhood concept

## Decision to validate

Does the Atlas improve recognition and emotional attachment without obscuring high-priority operational work or making the app feel childish?
